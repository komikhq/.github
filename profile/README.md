## About KomikHQ

KomikHQ is an engineering-driven organization dedicated to building high-performance infrastructure for digital comic consumption and delivery on the modern web.

Traditional digital comic platforms suffer from high latency, excessive layout shifts, and heavy resource overhead. KomikHQ eliminates these barriers through lean system engineering, globally distributed edge computation, and purpose-built architectures optimized for instantaneous content delivery.

---

## Engineering Pillars

**Edge-Distributed Architecture**
All computational workloads and caching layers are deployed as close to end users as possible, ensuring sub-second load times and instant page transitions across global regions.

**Reader-Centric Experience**
Every interface decision prioritizes fluid chapter navigation, zero layout disruption, and ergonomic reading across light and dark display modes.

**Type Discipline and System Integrity**
End-to-end type safety, modular separation of concerns, and strict adherence to modern web standards guarantee reliability at every layer of the stack.
