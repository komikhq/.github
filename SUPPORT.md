# Support Resources

Need assistance or have questions regarding **KomikHQ** projects? Here are the recommended ways to get help:

---

## Documentation First

Before opening an issue or reaching out, please check:
- The [README.md](profile/README.md) of the organization for high-level ecosystem context.
- The `README.md` and `docs/` folders inside the respective project repositories (`api`, `komikhq`, `komikhq-clipper`).

---

## Where to Get Help

| Need | Recommended Channel |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Found a bug?** | Open a [Bug Report](https://github.com/komikhq/.github/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml) on the relevant repository. |
| **Feature idea or enhancement?** | Open a [Feature Request](https://github.com/komikhq/.github/issues/new?template=feature_request.yml). |
| **Security issue?** | Review [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) and report privately. |
| **Internal team support?** | Check the internal guides in `.github-private` or team communication channels. |

---

## Scope of Support

KomikHQ maintainers provide support on a best-effort basis for code and architectures hosted within the organization. Please ensure issue reports include clear steps to reproduce and system environment details.
